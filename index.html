Guides & FAQ

PTE Academic is a computer-based English proficiency test accepted by universities, colleges and immigration authorities worldwide. It measures your Speaking, Writing, Reading and Listening skills in a single two-hour sitting, with AI-scored results delivered within days.

Faster results, fully computer-based scoring that removes human bias, and wide acceptance across universities and visa programs make it a popular choice for test takers on a deadline.

The test runs roughly two hours and is split into three main parts — Speaking & Writing, Reading, and Listening — each combining several question types that assess integrated skills.

Create a Pearson account, choose a nearby test centre and an available slot, then pay the test fee online. You can reschedule up to a few days before your appointment.
